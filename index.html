Product Architecture & Capabilities

How Briefloom Works

Designed for independent designers, software engineers, consultants, and boutique agencies who want commercial rigor without heavyweight enterprise bloat.

STAGE 01 • DISCOVERY INTAKE

Progressive Brief Capture

Instead of staring at a blank document after a client discovery call, Briefloom guides you through four structured steps: Client Foundation, Strategic Objectives, Deliverable Architecture, and Commercial Guardrails. Real-time completeness scoring highlights missing parameters before you price.

STAGE 02 • DUAL-MODE PRICING

Fixed & Hourly Hybrid Modeling

Creative and technical projects rarely fit a single billing model. Briefloom lets you price discovery workshops as fixed fees while estimating engineering sprints via hourly rates (`hours × hourly rate`), automatically summing core scope, optional add-ons, contingency buffers, discounts, and tax.

STAGE 03 • MILESTONE ALIGNMENT

Automated Tranche Math

Define project milestones by percentage allocation (such as 30% upon kickoff, 40% upon design approval, and 30% upon launch). Whenever a deliverable price or tax rate changes, every milestone payment amount updates automatically.

STAGE 04 • CLIENT HANDOVER

Swiss-Editorial PDF Export

Switch to the Proposal Preview tab to inspect your finished commercial proposal complete with reference numbers, objective callouts, itemized tables, milestone schedules, and acceptance signature blocks. Print directly to PDF or export a standalone HTML document.

Frequently Asked Questions & Reference

How are proposal totals and milestone payments calculated?

Each deliverable can be set to Fixed Price or Hourly Estimate (`Hours × Rate`). Items marked as Optional Add-on are only included in the subtotal when toggled active. Briefloom then applies your Contingency Buffer % and Partner Discount % to the net scope subtotal, and finally calculates Tax / VAT % on the adjusted subtotal. Each milestone receives its designated percentage of that grand total.

How do I export a clean PDF without browser headers or footers?

In the Proposal Studio, click Print / Export PDF. In your browser’s print dialog, select "Save as PDF" as the destination, and uncheck "Headers and footers" under More Settings for a clean editorial sheet. You can also click Export HTML to download a standalone, self-contained proposal document file.

Can I move my projects between computers or browsers?

Yes. Click Data I/O in the top navigation bar (or on the Workspace Dashboard) to download all saved projects as a `.json` backup file, or paste/upload a previously exported `.json` file to restore your workspace.

Privacy Information

Strict Local-First Data Architecture

Briefloom operates entirely inside your web browser. We do not require user accounts, cloud databases, or external telemetry servers.

  • ✓ 100% On-Device Persistence: Your projects, client names, financial estimates, and notes are stored in your browser’s `localStorage` under the key `briefloom_workspace_v2`.
  • ✓ Zero Server Transmission: No project briefs or commercial figures are transmitted to external servers.
  • ✓ Full Data Portability: Export your entire workspace as standard JSON at any time, or clear local browser storage with one click below.
